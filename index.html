Interpretation of absolute risks: These probabilities are calculated with the original derivation model without recalibration. In the external validation cohort, this model overpredicted absolute risk (observed-to-expected ratio 0.534 at 28 days and 0.620 at 90 days), so displayed probabilities may overestimate risk in other settings and may require local recalibration. The risk group reflects the patient’s ranking by score; it does not by itself establish the accuracy of the individual absolute risk shown.
Model used by this calculator (original derivation model; Fine-Gray subdistribution hazard model):
Score = 3.86×log10CRP − 4.28×Albumin + 4.92×INR + 14.35×log10Bilirubin + 10.68×Creatinine + 40
P(t) = 1 − exp(−H0(t)·exp(β·Score)), with β = 0.1047317, H0(28) = 0.0002188322, H0(90) = 0.000309139.
Absolute risks use the unrounded score; risk groups use the score rounded to one decimal place. No recalibration parameters are applied.
